ABOUT FERNLEAF

Building Bridges Between Markets

Fernleaf New Zealand Limited was established with a simple vision: to create trusted commercial bridges between New Zealand, India and international markets. We identify quality products, technologies, services and business opportunities and develop the partnerships, distribution networks and market channels required to take them across borders.
